The four classes

Glioma

Grows from glial cells inside the brain tissue itself — the most complex tumour type.

Meningioma

Arises from the meninges, the membranes covering the brain. Usually slow-growing and benign.

Pituitary

A growth in the pituitary gland beneath the brain — can affect hormones and vision.

No Tumor

No tumour found — the scan shows a healthy brain.
